01Connecting interface to applicationWeb development / INFO 2589Teaching experience

Alex's instruction progresses from guided TypeScript/React and Express tasks to an Events App assignment, testing, GitHub submissions, and screenshots of running work.

02Turning systems knowledge into practiceSystems administration / INFO 2451Curriculum design example

INFO 2451 materials cover Windows/Linux administration, permissions, Active Directory, network services, virtualization, security, and recovery. Related planning pairs guided labs with troubleshooting evidence.

Design focus: Connect configuration decisions to observable checks.

Illustrative activity

Diagnose an access-permission issue in an isolated training environment. Make a justified change and verify authorized access.

Evidence: Diagnosis, configuration records, and repeatable validation steps.

03Applying AI with practical judgmentApplied AI / Phased pathwayCourse proposal

Alex's proposed course combines AI fluency, prompting, output evaluation, process analysis, no-code automation, and responsible use. It proposes a local business or nonprofit project before a later technical course.

Design focus: Sequence practical judgment before technical depth.

Illustrative activity

Map a workflow with synthetic documents, prototype an AI-assisted step, and identify human-review points.

Evidence: A workflow map, prototype, and evaluation notes addressing accuracy and bias.

Before we begin

A few practical
questions.

Can we start with one course?

Yes. A refresh, new build, or focused review can be scoped independently.

How much faculty involvement is needed?

Faculty identify priorities, review changes, and approve academic content. We agree on reviewers, feedback windows, and handoff at the start so the work fits available capacity.

Can you work with our LMS?

We confirm platform, format, access, and implementation scope before committing.

Can we edit and reuse the materials?

We plan for editable deliverables and handoff. Ownership, licensing, and reuse terms are documented in the agreement, including third-party resources.

What is included in the engagement?

The proposal identifies courses, deliverables, review rounds, implementation support, and fee. A review provides findings and recommendations; rebuilding, custom tools, and integrations are included when specified. AI and cybersecurity services focus on curriculum, rather than enterprise security testing or compliance certification.

Do you work outside Nebraska?

Yes. We're available to colleges across Iowa, Kansas, Missouri, South Dakota, and the wider Midwest, with remote collaboration and on-campus sessions for regional institutions.
