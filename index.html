Services

Why Access
exists.

Alex Shaw and Baha Marupov are software professionals and college educators. While teaching at colleges, they saw a recurring challenge: technology changes quickly, but keeping courses current takes time that faculty must balance with teaching and supporting students.

Access exists to help with that work. We work alongside your faculty to update courses and programs, create new computing courses, and keep teaching materials current after a course is launched.

Three ways we can help

01

Review and revision project

Review and update courses and programs.

A defined project to identify what needs changing and complete agreed updates to an existing course or computing program.

  • Review content, tools, learning goals, prerequisites, and course sequence
  • Identify gaps and set priorities for improvement
  • Revise agreed teaching materials, projects, and assessments
Discuss a course or program ↗
02

New course project

Create a new computing course.

Develop a course from its initial purpose through the learning activities and materials faculty need to teach it.

  • Set learning goals, prerequisites, and a course plan
  • Build lessons, labs, projects, and assessments
  • Prepare teaching materials faculty can use and adapt
Discuss a new course ↗
03

Annual Curriculum Partnership

Keep courses and programs current.

Technology keeps changing after a course is launched. Our annual curriculum partnership helps your faculty keep computing courses and programs current. We review the curriculum each year, identify changes in technology and workplace needs, and make agreed updates to course materials, projects, and assessments.

  • Annual review, plus agreed revisions and faculty consultation during the year
  • Updated materials and a record of changes
  • Remaining priorities and a refreshed plan for the coming year
Discuss an annual partnership ↗

Annual partnership: Review together → agree priorities → update materials → review the changes with faculty.

Working with your faculty

College budgets can also make it difficult to match the salaries offered by technology companies. We offer help through a defined course or program project, or an annual partnership.

Before we begin, we agree on the courses or program covered, included revisions, faculty consultation, schedule, and fee. Additional work is scoped and agreed separately. Your faculty guide the work, make the academic decisions, and approve the final materials.
