Services

Why Access
exists.

Alex Shaw and Baha Marupov are software professionals and college educators. While teaching at colleges, they saw a recurring challenge: technology changes quickly, but keeping courses current takes time that faculty must balance with teaching and supporting students.

Access exists to help with that work. We work alongside your faculty to update existing computing courses or create new ones—with practical projects, clear assessments, and teaching materials your faculty can use and adapt.

Two ways we can help

01

Modernize an
existing course.

Bring tools, content, and assignments up to date while keeping the course's learning goals and student needs in view.

  • Review the syllabus and existing materials
  • Update technologies, examples, and applied projects
  • Refine assessments, rubrics, and instructor guidance
Discuss a course update ↗
02

Create a new
computing course.

Develop a course from its initial purpose through the learning activities and materials faculty need to teach it.

  • Set learning goals, prerequisites, and a course plan
  • Build lessons, labs, projects, and assessments
  • Prepare teaching materials faculty can use and adapt

Working with your faculty

College budgets can also make it difficult to match the salaries offered by technology companies. We offer help with a specific course project, with the work, timeline, and fee agreed in advance.

We develop the materials with faculty feedback and provide guidance for teaching the course. Your faculty make the academic decisions and approve the final materials.
