How We Help

While teaching at colleges, we saw how quickly technology changes and how course updates compete with teaching and student support for faculty time. College budgets can also make it difficult to match technology salaries. We work alongside faculty to close that gap and keep courses current after launch.

Defined project

Review and Update Courses and Programs

Identify what needs changing in an existing course or computing program and complete agreed updates.

  • Review content, tools, learning goals, prerequisites, and course sequence
  • Identify gaps and provide a prioritized improvement plan
  • Revise agreed teaching materials, projects, and assessments
Discuss a course or program

Defined project

Create a New Computing Course

Develop a course with faculty, from its learning goals to materials ready to teach.

  • Set learning goals, prerequisites, and a course plan
  • Build lessons, labs, projects, and assessments
  • Provide instructor guidance and materials faculty can adapt

Annual Curriculum Partnership

Keep Courses and Programs Current

Review technology and workplace needs each year, with agreed updates and faculty consultation throughout the year.

  • Annual review of the covered courses or program
  • Updated materials, projects, and assessments, with a record of changes
  • Remaining priorities and a refreshed plan for the coming year
Discuss an annual partnership

Annual partnership: Review together → agree priorities → update materials → review the changes with faculty.

Working with Your Faculty

We agree on the courses or program covered, revisions, faculty consultation, schedule, and fee before work begins. Additional work is scoped and agreed separately. Your faculty guide the work, make academic decisions, and approve the final materials.
